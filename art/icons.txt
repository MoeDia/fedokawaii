# art/icons.txt — the desktop's own pixel icons, one block per icon.
# Drawn for Sumi UI's 14 px grid (Shinonome Gothic 14, regular): '#' = pixel, '.' = empty.
# Weighted as its letters are: every stroke one pixel (docs/design-system.md §13).
#
# A block starts with "@name", optionally followed by settings:
#   adv=N   advance in pixels (default: the width rounded up to whole 7 px columns of the
#           font, so a 12 or 14 px icon takes two, its air shared either side)
#   x=N     where the drawing starts, in pixels from the pen (default: centred in adv)
# Every size even × even, its middle on the capitals' middle (Sumi UI's em rows 2–11), so
# beside words an icon sits as the letters do, at 14 px, 28 px, 42 px…:
#   12 × 12   beside words (on the em's rows 1–12)
#   14 × 14   alone: where no words follow (the layouts, a tile's picture) — the em's rows
#   16 × 16   the bar's three emblems: the torii, the stamp, quick settings' face
#   18 × 12   the wide ones: the volume's ladder, the cassette
#
# tools/build-icons.py turns this file into
#   configs/home/.local/share/fonts/SumiIcons.ttf       font "Sumi Icons", U+FE000 onwards
#                                                       in this order (Supplementary Private
#                                                       Use Area A, clear of every Nerd Font)
#   configs/home/.local/share/fonts/SumiIconsTerm.ttf   "Sumi Icons Term": the same, for the
#                                                       terminal's Sumi Term at 20 px
#   configs/home/.config/fedokawaii/icons.sh            ICON_<NAME> variables for the scripts
# Append new icons at the end: inserting one in the middle renumbers the ones after it.
