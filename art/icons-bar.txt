# art/icons-bar.txt — the bar's own icons (r28 sa25, his pick f 3 jikusaki), one block per icon.
# Drawn for the bar's 16 px grid (Shinonome 16: capitals 13 tall): '#' = pixel, '.' = empty.
# Every stroke two pixels; the air inside a drawing the same on both sides of it.
#
# The bar's one set of metrics (docs/design-system.md §8): the paper's 30 rows, the line on its
# middle 20 (16 up from the baseline, 4 down); an icon 20 × 20 on that line, its middle 6 px over
# the baseline (half a pixel under the capitals' middle: the sheet he picked), the emblems 24 × 24.
#
# Each block is named as the icon of art/icons.txt it stands for on the bar: tools/build-icons.py
# writes it into configs/home/.local/share/fonts/SumiIconsBar.ttf ("Sumi Icons Bar", 1600 units
# per em, 100 a pixel: at 16 px every pixel lands on one screen pixel) at that icon's bar twin
# (ICON_BAR_* and ICON_*_BAR in icons.sh), its ink from the pen, no air: the scripts stay as they
# are; the bar's style puts this font before Sumi Icons. The gaps (gap, gap1) come along at 2 and 1 px.
