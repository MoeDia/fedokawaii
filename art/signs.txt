# art/signs.txt — the signs Sumi UI and Sumi Term draw themselves (tools/build-fonts.py).
#
# The faces draw these two cells wide (Japanese text: an arrow, a circle, a dash takes a
# kanji's room) or not at all, or draw them the old way; a terminal and kawa-panel give each
# one cell. So the ones the desktop shows are drawn here, for each face's cell, in its weight,
# on its lines (rows as Pillow draws the face, from the top of its ascent):
#   ui     7 × 14: Shinonome Gothic 14 (regular: every stroke one pixel) — the em on rows
#                  1–14, baseline under row 12, small letters rows 6–12, capitals 3–12, the
#                  minus and the plus's middle on row 8, the full stop 2 × 2 on rows 11–12
#   term  10 × 20: the line rows 1–20, baseline under row 17, small letters rows 8–17,
#                  capitals 4–17, the minus on row 10, the full stop rows 15–17 (Kappa 20 Bold:
#                  upright strokes two pixels, the rest one)
# "= SIGN FACE ROW": the drawing below starts on that row of the line; '#' ink, '.' paper,
# each line exactly the cell's width. The rightmost column stays paper (the next letter's
# air), except where a sign is meant to run on (—, ―; the box lines are foot's own).
# A letter can be redrawn here too (ui: T, whose bar filled the cell and touched its
# neighbours: 52 pairs).
