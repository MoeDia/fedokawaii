# art/signs.txt — the signs Sumi UI draws itself (tools/build-fonts.py).

# The face draws these two cells wide (Japanese text: an arrow, a circle, a dash takes a
# kanji's room) or not at all, or draws them the old way; a terminal and kawa-panel give each
# one cell. So the ones the desktop shows are drawn here, for its cell, in its weight, on its
# lines (rows as Pillow draws the face, from the top of its ascent): 7 × 14, Shinonome Gothic
# 14 (regular: every stroke one pixel) — the em on rows 1–14, baseline under row 12, small
# letters rows 6–12, capitals 3–12, the minus and the plus's middle on row 8, the full stop
# 2 × 2 on rows 11–12.
# "= SIGN ui ROW": the drawing below starts on that row of the line; '#' ink, '.' paper, each
# line exactly the cell's width. The rightmost column stays paper (the next letter's air),
# except where a sign is meant to run on (—, ―).
# A letter can be redrawn here too (T, whose bar filled the cell and touched its neighbours).
