# art/cursors.txt — the mouse pointers, "Sumi", drawn pixel by pixel (tools/build-cursors.py).
# An ink brush in crisp pixels: the arrow a stroke with a dry-brush streak, the busy pointer
# an ensō being drawn. Ink and its rim only — vermilion is kept for "not here" alone: the seal.
#
# Every shape is drawn twice, on the 24 px grid and on the 32 px grid; at 1:1 every
# character is one screen pixel (48 px, for 2× screens, is the 24 px drawing doubled).
# A drawing: "@name 24|32" and its settings, then its rows, then an empty line:
#   hot=X,Y        the hotspot: the pixel that points (column, row, from 0)
#   halo           a one-pixel rim is added all round its ink (leave a clear edge for it)
#   over=NAME      laid over NAME's drawing on the same grid (its hotspot, unless hot=)…
#   at=X,Y         …with this drawing's top left there
#   turn=NAME      NAME's drawing turned a quarter, clockwise (hotspot and all: no rows)
#   mirror=NAME    NAME's drawing, left for right (hotspot and all: no rows)
#   anim           animated: the stroke is drawn in the order its characters count
#                  (1-9, then a-z), in ink over the last one's ghost in wash; then held
# Colours — each variant takes them from its own theme's palette (tools/build-themes.py):
#   .  clear      #  ink         o  rim          :  wash (thinned ink)
#   R  vermilion                                (an anim drawing: its steps only)
#                 Sumi-dark                     Sumi-light
#   ink           paper (strong)  #ece3cc       sumi (strong)   #0f0e0c
#   rim           sumi (ground)   #0f0e0c       paper (hi)      #fbf7ec
# The other names programs ask by are at the end ("= drawn  other names…").
