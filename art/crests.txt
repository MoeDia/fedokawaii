# art/crests.txt — quick settings' pictures: one per hour of the dial and per action in its
# corners (docs/design-system.md §4, §13). '#' = pixel, '.' = empty; every picture 28 × 28.
#
# The grammar: one ink — the panel paints it in the state's colour (an hour: accent on, muted
# off; an action: muted; under the cursor's lacquer: text_on_select; in the dial's middle twice
# as large, accent on, text off). Strokes 2 px both ways; a 45° stroke a run of 3 on the stair
# (it reads as heavy as a straight 2); details knocked out 1 px wide; masses solid. Drawn first
# as crests (kamon, 家紋), the ink stays inside a circle 26 across. Centred on the 28 px square,
# the odd pixel of air to the right and below (as everywhere: a word of odd width in an even
# cell). A line across a picture means "off" (bell-off) — no other picture has one.
#
# The motifs: the theme's moon and star (月に星, dark) and sun (light); a stone lantern (灯籠,
# the night light); water breaking into a curl (freesync); the network's socket, waves, and
# waves with a cross (offline); the bluetooth rune; headphones (its devices); a USB stick
# (drives); a senryō-bako, the iron-bound chest a house kept its gold in (backup); a bonshō,
# the temple bell, and the bell struck through (dnd); a spool and its needle — the saver is
# sashiko (kawa-saver); a cine camera (record — record-2, its reels turned an eighth: the two
# frames kawa-panel turns while it records); a region's corners round three lines (read).
# The corners' actions: a still camera, its lens a ring of air (screenshot); a framed print of
# Fuji under the sun (wallpaper); the monitor gone dark, the power sign in it (screen off);
# the power sign (power).
#
# tools/build-icons.py turns each into configs/home/.local/share/fedokawaii/crests/NAME.png
# (white, its alpha the drawing: a mask), which kawa-panel paints; NAME is the item's icon
# without "tile_", "_" as "-" (tile_bell_off → bell-off, screen_off → screen-off).
