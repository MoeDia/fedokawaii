# art/crests.txt — quick settings' crests: one picture per switch, drawn for the mon sheet's
# seal (docs/design-system.md §4, §13). '#' = pixel, '.' = empty; every picture 28 × 28.
#
# The grammar (kamon, 家紋): one ink — the panel paints it in the state's colour (on: knocked
# out of the vermilion seal in `key`; off: `muted` inside the seal's outline; under the cursor:
# `select` on the cream seal, or `text_on_select`). Strokes 2 px both ways; a 45° stroke a run
# of 3 on the stair (it reads as heavy as a straight 2); details knocked out 1 px wide; masses
# solid. The ink stays inside a circle 26 across (every inked pixel's centre within 13 of the
# middle): 2 px of vermilion before the seal's paper ring. Centred on the 28 px square, the odd
# pixel of air to the right and below (as everywhere: a word of odd width in an even cell).
# A line across a picture means "off" (bell-off) — no other picture has one.
#
# The motifs: the theme's moon and star (月に星, dark) and sun (light); a stone lantern (灯籠,
# the night light); water breaking into a curl (freesync); the network's socket, waves, and
# waves with a cross (offline); the bluetooth rune; headphones (its devices); a USB stick
# (drives); a senryō-bako, the iron-bound chest a house kept its gold in (backup); a bonshō,
# the temple bell, and the bell struck through (dnd); a spool and its needle — the saver is
# sashiko (kawa-saver); a cine camera (record); a region's corners round three lines (read).
#
# tools/build-icons.py turns each into configs/home/.local/share/fedokawaii/crests/NAME.png
# (white, its alpha the drawing: a mask), which kawa-panel paints; NAME is the tile's icon
# without "tile_" (tile_bell_off → bell-off).
