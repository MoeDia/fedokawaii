## idea
The bar runs across the top, made of tiles of paper. From the left: the torii, which opens the launcher; the workspaces in kanji numerals, the one you are on under a vermilion seal; the layout icon; the focused window's title. The date and time hang in the middle, as a scroll. On the right: the music playing, drives, memory, volume, brightness, the weather, and last the cat, which opens quick settings. A click on most tiles opens a panel under it; point at one and it turns teal.

## exercise
Click the date: the calendar drops down; Esc closes it. Click the time and pick 5 min: an hourglass counts down beside it. Point at the volume and turn the mouse wheel: it moves two at a time. Click the cat, then Esc.
