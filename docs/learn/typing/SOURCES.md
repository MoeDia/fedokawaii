# Typing: sources

All read on 4 November 2026. What each one decided: `.claude/notes/typing-research.md`.

## How typing is taught
- TypingClub / EdClub, *Typing Jungle Teacher Handbook* (sections, keys, min and goal speeds, syllabus,
  tips): https://login.typingclub.com/m/edclubdocs/media/pdf/typing-jungle-handbook.pdf
- EdClub help, glossary (speed goal, perfect score, accuracy, real accuracy):
  https://www.edclub.com/help/faq/glossary.html
- keybr.com, source (AGPL-3.0: read for the idea, nothing copied):
  https://github.com/aradzie/keybr.com — `packages/keybr-lesson/lib/guided.ts`, `target.ts`, `settings.ts`,
  `packages/keybr-result/lib/keystats.ts`

## How it is measured
- Monkeytype, source (GPL-3.0: read for the definitions, nothing copied):
  https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype — `frontend/src/ts/test/test-logic.ts`,
  `frontend/src/ts/test/events/stats.ts`, `frontend/src/ts/utils/numbers.ts`, `packages/util/src/numbers.ts`
- R. W. Soukoreff, I. S. MacKenzie, *Metrics for text entry research: an evaluation of MSD and KSPC, and a
  new unified error metric*, CHI 2003: https://www.yorku.ca/mack/chi03.html
- foot's terminal sequences (Kitty keyboard, modes 2026, 2031, sixel, 8452): `man foot-ctlseqs`, foot 1.27.0

## Studies
- V. Dhakal, A. M. Feit, P. O. Kristensson, A. Oulasvirta, *Observations on Typing from 136 Million
  Keystrokes*, CHI 2018, https://doi.org/10.1145/3173574.3174220 (PDF:
  https://www.pokristensson.com/pubs/DhakalEtAlCHI2018.pdf)
- A. M. Feit, D. Weir, A. Oulasvirta, *How We Type: Movement Strategies and Performance in Everyday
  Typing*, CHI 2016: https://userinterfaces.aalto.fi/how-we-type/
- E. Yechiam, I. Erev, V. Yehene, D. Gopher, *Melioration and the Transition from Touch-Typing Training to
  Everyday Use*, Human Factors 45(4), 2003: https://yeldad.net.technion.ac.il/files/2016/12/type.pdf
- A. D. Baddeley, D. J. A. Longman, *The Influence of Length and Frequency of Training Session on the Rate
  of Learning to Type*, Ergonomics 21(8), 1978:
  https://www.gwern.net/doc/psychology/spaced-repetition/1978-baddeley.pdf
- N. Keith, K. A. Ericsson, *A Deliberate Practice Account of Typing Proficiency in Everyday Typists*, Journal of
  Experimental Psychology: Applied 13(3), 2007, https://doi.org/10.1037/1076-898X.13.3.135 (preprint:
  https://www.humanw.tu-darmstadt.de/media/orgpsy/keithericsson2007_preprint.pdf)
- K. A. Ericsson, R. T. Krampe, C. Tesch-Römer, *The role of deliberate practice in the acquisition of expert
  performance*, Psychological Review 100, 1993.
- G. D. Logan, M. J. C. Crump, the two-loop theory of typewriting (2011), as described in M. J. C. Crump,
  G. D. Logan, *Hierarchical control and skilled typing*, 2010:
  https://www.crumplab.com/publications/ ; A. Yamaguchi, G. D. Logan, 2016:
  https://repository.essex.ac.uk/26184/
- A. W. Salmoni, R. A. Schmidt, C. B. Walter, *Knowledge of results and motor learning*, Psychological
  Bulletin 95, 1984; C. J. Winstein, R. A. Schmidt, *Reduced frequency of knowledge of results enhances motor
  skill learning*, JEP: LMC 16, 1990 (both as cited in https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2010.00190).
- T. A. Salthouse, *Perceptual, cognitive, and motoric aspects of transcription typing*, Psychological
  Bulletin 99, 1986 (its 29 phenomena; the abstract only was read).
