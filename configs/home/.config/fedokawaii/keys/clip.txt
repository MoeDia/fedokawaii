# clipboard (Super+V): what you copied, text and pictures (cliphist), the newest first
# source: bin/kawa-clip (its fzf keys: enter, esc, ctrl-d, ctrl-x) and keybinds.txt (Super+V, Super+Z)
── clipboard: Super+V ─────────────────────────────────────────────
type                    find in what you copied
↑ / ↓                   the one before / after (shown below)
Enter                   copy it again (it goes on top), and away
Ctrl+D                  forget this one
Ctrl+X                  forget all of it
Super+Z                 shrink or convert what you copied
Esc / Super+V           away
