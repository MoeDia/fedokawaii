# bluetui 0.8.2 (pinned in lib/upstream.sh): Bluetooth pairing from quick settings
# command: bluetui --version
# source: https://github.com/pythops/bluetui/blob/v0.8.2/Readme.md (the rebindable keys, with their defaults)
# source: https://github.com/pythops/bluetui/blob/v0.8.2/src/handler.rs (the key handling) and src/help.rs (its help line)
# ours: configs/home/.config/bluetui/config.toml (esc_quit = true)
── bluetui: moving ────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tab / Shift+Tab         next / previous list (l / h too)
j / k                   next / previous device (arrow keys too)
Esc or q                close (Esc leaves a question first, such as a PIN)
── bluetui: devices ───────────────────────────────────────────────
s                       scan for new devices, on or off
Enter or Space          pair a found device · (dis)connect a paired one
u                       unpair (asks first: Tab chooses, Enter confirms)
t                       trust: it connects by itself next time
f                       favourite, on or off
e                       rename
── bluetui: the adapter ───────────────────────────────────────────
o                       power on or off
d                       discoverable (visible to others), on or off
p                       pairable (accepts pairing), on or off
