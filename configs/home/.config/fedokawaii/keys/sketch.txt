# FireAlpaca 2.16.3 (the official Linux build, lib/upstream.sh): Sketch, drawing and painting
# command: kawa-sketch
# source: the program's own menus and status line (File, Edit, Layer, Tool, View, Window: firealpaca.com/download, 2.16.3)
# ours: F2 to F7 (src/kawa-firealpaca PANEL_KEYS); the pen's buttons are its own Right Button and hand
── sketch: draw ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
B                       the brush
E                       the eraser
I                       the eyedropper (the pen's upper button, held)
H                       the hand (the pen's lower button, held; Space, held)
G  ·  N                 the bucket  ·  fill
V                       move a layer
Shift+B                 the dot brush
T                       text
Ctrl+Alt, drag          brush size
X  ·  D                 swap the colours  ·  black and white again
── sketch: the page ───────────────────────────────────────────────
Ctrl+Z  ·  Ctrl+Y       undo  ·  redo
Ctrl+S  ·  Ctrl+Shift+S keep the file  ·  a copy under a new name
Ctrl+N  ·  Ctrl+O       a new page  ·  open a file
Ctrl+0                  the whole page in the window
Ctrl++  ·  Ctrl+-       zoom
Left  ·  Right          turn the page
Down                    flip the page
── sketch: layers and selections ──────────────────────────────────
Ctrl+Shift+N            a new layer
Ctrl+J  ·  Ctrl+E       duplicate  ·  merge down
Ins  ·  Del             fill  ·  clear the layer
M  ·  L  ·  W           select: a box  ·  lasso  ·  magic wand
Ctrl+L  ·  Ctrl+U       levels  ·  hue
── sketch: the panels ─────────────────────────────────────────────
Tab                     every panel away, and back
F2  ·  F3               the navigator  ·  brush sizes
F4  ·  F5               brush preview  ·  colour history
F6  ·  F7               reference picture  ·  status bar
Ctrl+K                  the program's own settings
