# kawa-torrents: the desktop's own (bin/kawa-torrents); libtorrent 2.0.11 (Fedora's rb_libtorrent-python3), Prowlarr finds
# command: kawa-torrents --check
# source: configs/home/.local/lib/fedokawaii/kawa-torrents/app.py (key_list, key_files, key_find, key_adding)
── torrents: anywhere ─────────────────────────────────────────────
Tab                     downloads or find (Left / Right too; a click)
Ctrl+Q                  close the window: what downloads goes on
Paste                   a magnet link, a link or a .torrent: added at once
Ctrl+V                  the same, from the clipboard
── torrents: downloads ────────────────────────────────────────────
j / k                   move down / up (arrow keys too)
Ctrl+D / Ctrl+U         a page down / up
g / G                   top / bottom of the list
Enter or l              its details and files
a                       add: a magnet, a link, a path, a hash
y                       copy its magnet link
v                       play it now, while it comes
o                       its folder in yazi
p                       pause or go on (P: all of them)
r                       ask its trackers again, or try a failed one again
c                       check its files again
d / D                   remove it / remove it and delete its files
t                       slow: the speed held down, or free again
/                       only those that contain a word
q                       close the window
── torrents: adding ───────────────────────────────────────────────
Enter                   add what is on the line
Ctrl+O                  pick a .torrent in yazi
x                       an offer from the clipboard or Downloads: not now
── torrents: its files ────────────────────────────────────────────
Space                   keep it or skip it
a / n / i               keep all / none / the other way round
Enter                   play it now, while it comes
Esc                     back to the list
── torrents: find ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Enter                   search; on a result, download it
/                       back to the line
y                       copy its magnet link
s                       sort: seeds, size, newest, name
