# asobi (Super+U → system → asobi, the launcher's ASOBI): the play world, from the desktop
# source: bin/kawa-asobi (its fzf keys: enter, esc; on a snapshot d), src/dwl/config.h, system/asobi/xkb-symbols
── asobi: Super+G ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Super+G                 into the play world; in there, back here
↑ / ↓                   the row before / after
Enter                   do it
d                       on a snapshot: delete it (clean always stays)
Esc                     back; on the first screen, away
