── mpv: playing ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
Space or p              pause / play
Left / Right            back / forward 5 s (Shift: 1 s, exactly)
Down / Up               back / forward 1 minute
< / >                   previous / next file in the playlist
[ / ]                   play slower / faster (Backspace: normal speed)
f                       full screen (a double click too)
m                       mute
9 / 0                   volume down / up (the mouse wheel too)
q / Q                   quit / quit and remember the place
── mpv: subtitles and sound ───────────────────────────────────────
j / J                   next / previous subtitle track
v                       show or hide the subtitles
z / Z                   subtitles 0.1 s earlier / later
Ctrl+Left / Right       jump to the previous / next subtitle line
the # key               next audio track
Ctrl+- / Ctrl++         sound 0.1 s earlier / later (to match the picture)
g s / g a               pick a subtitle / audio track from a list
── mpv: more ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
s / S                   screenshot with / without the subtitles
l                       loop between two points: l for start, end, off
L                       loop the whole file, on or off
i / I                   statistics: for a moment / until switched off
g p                     pick a playlist entry from a list
Ctrl+V                  add the link you copied to the playlist
g b                     search all of mpv's keys
?                       mpv's own list of keys
