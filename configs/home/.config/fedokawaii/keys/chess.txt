# chess (learning in Super+U): today's plan, lessons, puzzles, your games, your progress
# source: configs/home/.local/lib/fedokawaii/kawa-chess (Home.key, MoveInput, Game.key, Session.key, Review.key)
── chess: on the board ────────────────────────────────────────────
Nf3 exd5 O-O            type a move: lower case and no x are fine
Enter                   play what is typed, or what the cursor is on
↑ ↓ ← →                 the keyboard's place on the board
a click                 pick up a piece, put it down
?                       a hint: the lantern on the piece, again the arrow
t                       marks on your pieces in danger, on or off
Esc                     let go of what is in hand; then the game's menu
q r b n                 which piece a pawn becomes (Enter: the queen)
── chess: the lists ───────────────────────────────────────────────
Tab / Shift+Tab         the next tab, the one before
← / →                   the same
↑ / ↓                   the row before, the row after
Enter                   do what the row says
Esc                     back; on the first screen, away
── chess: in a lesson ─────────────────────────────────────────────
Enter or Space          on to the next page
←                       the page before
↑ / ↓                   scroll a long page
── chess: in a review ─────────────────────────────────────────────
← / →                   walk the game; walk the better line
Enter                   choose your worst move; on to the next moment
Esc                     show the answer; skip what is left to write
