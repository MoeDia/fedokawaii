# books (Super+R): your books in ~/books, read in zathura — the ones you are reading first
# source: bin/kawa-books (its fzf keys: enter, ctrl-a, ctrl-d; --add and --remove)
── books: Super+R ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
type                    find a book by its title
↑ / ↓                   the one before / after (its cover beside)
Enter                   read it in zathura, where you stopped
Ctrl+A                  add books from ~/downloads
Ctrl+D                  remove it (to the trash)
Esc / Super+R           away
